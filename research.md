<div class="project" markdown="1">
## Description Length of Language and Task in Frozen Language Models
<p class="meta">Individual research project. <a href="https://github.com/sirotenkogeorgii/prefix-programs/blob/prefixlab/report/report.pdf">Report</a> and <a href="https://github.com/sirotenkogeorgii/prefix-programs">code</a></p>

Measures model-relative task complexity in a frozen Qwen2.5-1.5B by treating
variational soft prefixes as programs and coding them in bits (bits-back),
across translation, factual recall and continuation in three languages.
Translation programs take roughly 500 bits, and Russian factual-recall programs
cost about five times more. Sharing task and language components
hierarchically gave no consistent compression gain. Activation patching
recovered most translation-prefix effects in early layers, while recall-prefix
effects were spread across depth.
</div>
