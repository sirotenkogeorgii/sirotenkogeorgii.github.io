\newcommand{\PrefixProgramsResearchPhD}{%
\needspace{8\baselineskip}
\cvdated{Description Length of Language and Task in Frozen Language Models}{}
\textit{Individual research project} \quad\href{https://sirotenkogeorgii.github.io/assets/description-length-report.pdf}{[report]}\,\href{https://github.com/sirotenkogeorgii/prefix-programs}{[code]}\par
\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=1.35em,topsep=1pt,itemsep=1pt,parsep=0pt,partopsep=0pt]
\item Measured model-relative task complexity in a frozen Qwen2.5-1.5B, treating variational soft prefixes as programs and coding them in bits (bits-back), across translation, factual recall, and continuation in three languages.
\item Found translation programs of roughly 500 bits and Russian factual-recall programs about five times costlier; hierarchical sharing of task and language components gave no consistent compression gain. Activation patching recovered most translation-prefix effects in early layers, but recall-prefix effects were more distributed across depth.
\end{itemize}
\vspace{1pt}
